Draw one loop. Match every number.

Connect the dots into one closed fence. Each number tells you how many sides of that cell belong to the fence.

All rules & practice options

Connect adjacent dots with horizontal or vertical edges to form one continuous closed loop. It cannot branch, cross itself, or split into separate loops. You do not need to use every dot.

A number tells you exactly how many of that cell’s four sides belong to the fence. A blank cell has no number constraint. Crosses are optional notes for edges you have ruled out; you do not need to mark every unused edge.

Choose Fence, Cross, or Erase, then tap an edge. Tapping a matching mark removes it. Right-click an edge to toggle a cross. Undo and Redo preserve your drawing history. Submit checks your loop without revealing the solution after a wrong answer. On a phone, taps snap to the nearest edge; dots and ambiguous corner taps are ignored. Give up & reveal keeps your attempt for comparison. Cells whose borders differ pulse briefly; use Your attempt and Correct loop to compare. Jade marks missing edges and coral marks extra edges. Crosses are optional notes and are never counted as fence edges.

Keyboard: Tab to the board, then use arrow keys to move between edges. Enter or Space uses your selected tool; X toggles a cross; Delete or Backspace erases. Tab leaves the board. The board has one verified solution.

Difficulty changes grid size and clue density: Easy 4×4, Medium 5×5, Hard 6×6, Advanced 7×7. Levels describe puzzle structure, not measured solving times. A new puzzle or page reload discards the current drawing.
