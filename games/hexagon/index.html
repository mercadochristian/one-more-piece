    Game settings

    Sound and Beans changes apply immediately. Study and recall timers pause while dialogs are open or the tab is hidden.

    Game soundsTile clicks, phase changes and result sounds.

    Remember the numbers. Choose the letters.

    Memorize the numbered positions. After they hide, choose three adjacent tiles in one straight line whose remembered numbers add up to the target.

    All rules & practice options

    Every round has 19 tiles. Start memorizing when ready; the target stays hidden until recall. When the timer ends, or you choose Hide numbers, the board shows letters instead of numbers.

    Choose exactly three different neighboring tiles in one straight line. Horizontal and both diagonal directions count. You cannot turn a corner or skip an intervening tile. Submit their letters in any selection order.

    Find all 2–5 matching lines for the target. A correct new line earns 1 point. A wrong answer or repeated line costs 1 point. An incorrect submission preserves your selection; it does not reveal the hidden values.

    Easy: numbers 1–5, 60 seconds to memorize, untimed recall. Medium: 1–9, 30 seconds, untimed recall. Hard: 1–9, 20 seconds, 90-second recall. Advanced: 1–12, 12 seconds, 60-second recall. These are solo practice settings, not measured human difficulty guarantees.

    Timers pause while any dialog is open or this tab is hidden. Paused study numbers are covered. Use End round & reveal to see all values and matching lines. Difficulty changes and leaving an unfinished round ask for confirmation.

    Keyboard: Tab to the board during recall; arrow keys move between tiles. Enter or Space selects/deselects. Tab leaves the board. Three selected letters appear in the answer bar.
